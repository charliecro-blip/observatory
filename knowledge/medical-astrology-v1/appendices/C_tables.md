# Appendix C — Quick-Reference Correspondence Tables

## Purpose

This appendix consolidates the most-used correspondence tables from the reference into a single quick-reference location. These tables are also embedded in the individual entries where they're most relevant; this appendix gathers them together for at-a-glance use.

Tables included:

1. **Planets** — qualities, sect, body region, organs, day rulership.
2. **Signs** — element, modality, ruler, exaltation, detriment, fall, body region.
3. **Houses** — field, body region (via natural-zodiac inheritance), strength category, medical weight.
4. **Aspects** — degrees, orb, character.
5. **The four temperaments** — qualities, element, humor, season, planetary correspondences.
6. **Composite temperament method — factor weights.**
7. **Planetary day-and-hour** — the week and the Chaldean sequence.
8. **Dignities and debilities** — point-weight table.
9. **TCM Five Element / Western planet** — cross-tradition mapping.
10. **Sympathy and antipathy** — herbal cultivation logic.

## 6. Composite temperament method — factor weights

| # | Factor | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ascendant sign | 4 |
| 2 | Other planets in 1st house | 2 each |
| 3 | Ascendant ruler — sign quality | 3 |
| 4 | Ascendant ruler — modality | 1 |
| 5 | Planets aspecting Ascendant (within orb) | 1 each |
| 6 | Moon sign | 3 |
| 7 | Moon phase | 2 |
| 8 | Planets aspecting Moon (within orb) | 1 each |
| 9 | Season of birth | 2 |
| 10 | Lord of the Geniture | 2 |
| 11 | Almuten of the chart | 2 |

Clarity-gating thresholds:
- **Combination gap**: highest combination > next-highest by at least **20%**.
- **Single-quality gap**: dominant quality > opposite by at least **30%**.

Both must be cleared to name a temperament.

## 7. Planetary day-and-hour

### Days of the week

| Day | Ruler | Energetic register |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Sun | Solar — central vitality, visibility, generosity |
| Monday | Moon | Lunar — rhythm, receptivity, cyclic feeling |
| Tuesday | Mars | Martial — activation, drive, heat |
| Wednesday | Mercury | Mercurial — communication, motion, exchange |
| Thursday | Jupiter | Jovial — expansion, broadening, generosity |
| Friday | Venus | Venusian — pleasure, ease, relational warmth |
| Saturday | Saturn | Saturnian — structure, discipline, gravity |

### Chaldean order (used in planetary hours)

The planets cycle through the hours in this order:

**Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Sun → Venus → Mercury → Moon → (repeat)**

The first planetary hour after sunrise is always the day's ruler. The cycle continues through 12 daylight hours (with hour-length varying by season) and 12 nighttime hours (similarly variable).

## 10. Sympathy and antipathy — herbal cultivation logic

| Constitutional pattern | Sympathy (like supports like) | Antipathy (opposite tempers excess) |
|---|---|---|
| Saturn-prominent (Cold+Dry, melancholic) | Saturn plants in moderation (mineral-toning, structural) | Sun and Jupiter plants (warming, broadening) |
| Jupiter-prominent (Hot+Moist, sanguine register) | Jupiter plants (hepatic-supportive, expansive) | Saturn and Mercury plants (focusing, draining) |
| Mars-prominent (Hot+Dry, choleric) | Mars plants for directed expression (warming-pungent) | Venus and Moon plants (cooling-moistening) |
| Sun-prominent (Hot+Dry, central-vital) | Sun plants (cardiotonic-warming, central support) | Saturn plants (cooling, structural counterbalance) |
| Venus-prominent (Cold+Moist, relational-pleasure) | Venus plants (sweet, moistening, fertility-supportive) | Mars plants (warming, active) for excess passivity |
| Mercury-prominent (Variable, nervous-air) | Mercury plants (nervine-aromatic-quick) | Jupiter or Saturn plants for excess scattering |
| Moon-prominent (Cold+Moist, cyclic-fluid) | Moon plants (cooling, fluid-supportive, lymphatic) | Sun plants (warming, drying for fluid excess) |
