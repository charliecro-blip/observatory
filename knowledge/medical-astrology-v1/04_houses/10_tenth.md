# The Tenth House — Career, Public Role, the Medicine

**Field:** career, public role, authority, the mother (in some traditions), the highest point of the chart, the medicine or treatment (in medical reading)
**Body register:** knees and joints (by inheritance from Capricorn-as-natural-tenth-sign); the body in public expression
**Angular** · **Full medical depth — the "medicine itself" house**

> *"The tenth house giveth judgment of kings, princes, dukes, earls, judges, prime officers, commanders in chief, of estimation, profession, public character."* — Lilly, *Christian Astrology*

> *"In a question of physic, the tenth house and the lord thereof do represent the medicine, the kind of physic to be administered."* — Culpeper, *Astrological Judgement of Diseases*

---

## What the tenth house is

The tenth house is the **angular field of the public role** — the Midheaven (MC), the highest point of the chart, the most visible position. Career, public character, authority, reputation, what the person is known for; in some traditions, the mother (with the father going to the fourth); in horary, the king or person of authority being asked about.

In medical reading, the tenth house has a distinctive role: it is the **house of the medicine itself** — the treatment, the cure, the *materia* being applied to the disease. Where the first house is the patient, the sixth is the disease, the seventh is the physician, the tenth is **what the physician administers**. This four-part structure (patient / disease / physician / medicine) is the classical decumbiture chart's organizing framework, and the tenth house is its medicine-anchor.

It is **angular**, with full chart weight, and inherits its body-region attribution from Capricorn (knees, joints, skeletal frame, skin).

---

## Tradition

The tenth-house medical doctrine has several layers:

**1. The medicine or treatment.** In horary and decumbiture work, the tenth house and its ruler signify the **medicine being applied** — the treatment, the remedy, the *materia medica* administered to the patient. The condition of the tenth-house ruler reports on the medicine's likely effect: strong placement, supportive treatment; afflicted placement, treatment mismatched to the disease.

**2. The 7th-vs-10th attribution puzzle (revisited).** Sources split on whether the physician is signified by the 7th house or the 10th. Observatory's working practice (documented in the 7th-house entry): **7th house for the physician as person; 10th house for the medicine or treatment itself.** This resolves the doctrinal split by giving each house a distinct medical role.

**3. The patient-medicine relationship.** The aspect between the first-house lord (patient) and the tenth-house lord (medicine) is read in decumbiture work as the relationship between the body and the treatment: applying aspects suggest the treatment is reaching the body; separating aspects, the treatment may not be matching. **Symbolic only** in Observatory — not used as treatment recommendation.

**4. The knees and joints — Capricorn inheritance.** The body-region attribution from Capricorn brings knee, joint, skin, and skeletal-frame register into the tenth house's territory. Stronger when Capricorn is also engaged.

**5. Career and public role as constitutionally relevant.** The work one does publicly, the role one holds, the conditions of public exposure — all of these affect the body. The tenth house's career-and-authority register has direct medical implication in natal reading: what does the body labor at publicly? Does the public role support or deplete the body?

**6. The mother (in some traditions).** Some classical sources assign the mother to the tenth house and the father to the fourth; others reverse this. Modern psychological astrology often treats the assignment as gender-neutral. Observatory documents the disagreement and does not enforce a fixed assignment.

**Conditions traditionally noted from tenth-house emphasis:** knee-and-joint register (via Capricorn inheritance), conditions related to public role and career (the body shaped by what it is publicly known for), conditions of skin and skeletal frame (Capricorn inheritance).

## Mixed testimony

**Medicine vs. physician.** The split between 7th and 10th for the physician/medicine attribution is one of the cleanest doctrinal disagreements in classical medical astrology. The Observatory resolution (7th = person, 10th = treatment) is one valid synthesis; other practitioners assign both to the 7th, or use the 10th for "the medicine that works" specifically. Documented as Charlie's working choice.

**Mother vs. father.** Same kind of disagreement as the fourth house entry. Observatory documents without enforcing.

**Public role and the body.** Modern psychological astrology has expanded tenth-house reading into the broader register of vocational fit and meaning-of-work. Observatory uses this carefully — the body shaped by its public role is a real medical reading; predicting career outcomes or judging vocational choices is not.

**Knees and joints inheritance.** Same soft-inheritance pattern as other body-region inheritances. Stronger when Capricorn is also engaged.

## When tenth-house emphasis is a resource

A well-emphasized tenth house gives **public-role fluency**:

- The body knows its public expression; the role-and-career register is constitutionally supported.
- Authority and visibility are accessible without depletion.
- Knee-and-joint register (when Capricorn-engaged) has Saturn's structural reliability.
- The "medicine" attribution in decumbiture — strong tenth-house in a question chart can suggest constitutionally supportive treatment dynamics.

---

## Counterbalance — when tenth-house emphasis is over-running

When the tenth house is heavily emphasized:

- The body becomes over-defined by public role; private and inherited registers (fourth house) suffer.
- Career and authority eclipse intimate and foundational life.
- Public exposure depletes the body; the constitutional cost of visibility outruns the supports.
- Knee-and-joint overload (when Capricorn engaged) from the constitutional tendency to bear weight publicly.

Counterbalance is the **fourth house** — home, foundation, ancestors, the private register. The structural opposition teaches the same.

## Cross-tradition note (TCM)

The tenth-house knees-and-joints inheritance from Capricorn maps onto **TCM Kidney (bones, joints)** via the same correspondence noted in the Capricorn entry. TCM does not have a "tenth house" or a direct medicine-attribution mechanism — its treatment frame is empirical (the practitioner selects herbs and acupuncture points based on pulse, tongue, and symptom pattern) rather than chart-based.

The "public role as constitutionally relevant" register is shared at the principle level — TCM treats lifestyle, including work conditions and social role, as constitutionally relevant — but does not have a positional-house mechanism for reading it.

## Safety flags ⚠

- **Treatment recommendation firewall.** Observatory does **not** use the tenth-house reading to recommend, prescribe, contraindicate, or evaluate specific medical treatments. The 10th-house attribution to "the medicine" is symbolic; it does not give the chart the right to comment on specific drugs, surgeries, therapies, or clinical decisions.
- **Career prediction firewall.** Observatory does not predict career outcomes, job success, vocational direction, or authority appointment from tenth-house placements.
- **Vocational judgment firewall.** Tenth-house emphasis is not used to judge whether someone has chosen the right work or to recommend career changes from chart placements.
- **Knee/joint disease firewall.** Soft tenth-house knee-and-joint inheritance via Capricorn does not generate clinical orthopedic diagnoses.
- **Parental claims caution.** The mother/father attribution (10th vs. 4th) is documented; not used to interpret family dynamics or to assess parental relationships.
- **Authority/public-figure caution.** The classical "tenth house signifies kings, princes, prime officers" content does not give Observatory the right to make claims about specific public figures.
