# The Sixth House — Illness, Daily Routine, Work

**Field:** illness, daily routine, work, servants (in classical), the body's daily conditions
**Body register:** intestines (by inheritance from Virgo-as-natural-sixth-sign); the body in the context of daily labor and routine
**Cadent** · **The classical house of sickness — full medical depth**

> *"The sixth house giveth judgment of diseases, sicknesses, infirmities."* — Lilly, *Christian Astrology*

> *"In all questions of sickness, the sixth house and his lord do represent the disease itself, the manner thereof, the part affected."* — Culpeper, *Astrological Judgement of Diseases*

---

## What the sixth house is

The sixth house is the **classical house of illness** — the field most directly tagged in the medical tradition. It is **cadent** (one of the three cadent houses, traditionally weak in dignity scoring), and the doctrine reads its cadency as symbolically appropriate to its content: illness is the body's falling-away from the angular vitality of the first house. Whether or not one accepts the symbolic logic, the sixth house's primacy in medical reading is universal across two millennia of tradition.

The sixth house's classical content extends well beyond sickness alone:

- **Illness itself** — diseases, infirmities, the conditions the body labors under.
- **Daily routine and work** — the conditions of the body's everyday labor, the work the body does, the conditions of that work.
- **Servants** — in the classical period, the household workers; in modern reading, the relationships of daily-life dependency that affect the body (employees, caretakers, daily helpers, the people whose work supports yours).
- **Small animals** — pets, livestock, the daily-life animal relationships.

All of these can affect the body. The unifying principle: the **sixth house is the field of the body's daily conditions** — what the body labors under, what it serves, what it depends on for the everyday.

Medically, this is the house Observatory reads most carefully and with the strictest safety floor. The classical tradition contains substantial prognostic and diagnostic content here — much of which Observatory adjusts heavily or excludes entirely.

## Tradition

The sixth-house medical doctrine has several layered components in the classical sources:

**1. The sixth house and its lord as significators of the disease.** When a chart is consulted about an illness — particularly a horary or decumbiture chart — the sixth house and its ruler stand for **the disease itself**, distinct from the patient (who is signified by the first house). The condition of the sixth-house ruler — its sign, house, dignity, aspects — is read for the nature of the disease, the body part affected, the disease's character, and (in older sources) its prognosis.

**2. The sixth-house sign and ruler as describing the disease's character.** The sign on the sixth-house cusp suggests what *kind* of disease — its element, quality, and humoral register. The planet ruling the sign suggests *which planetary register* — Saturn-coded diseases (cold, slow, chronic), Mars-coded (hot, acute, inflammatory), and so on. This material is preserved in Observatory's Workbench (practitioner-facing) and stripped of prognostic content for user-facing surfaces.

**3. The body part affected.** Some traditional sources read the sixth-house sign for the body part affected by the disease — using the standard sign-melothesia. A decumbiture chart with Aries on the sixth-house cusp would suggest a head-region condition; Pisces, a foot or fluid condition. This is symbolic interpretive layering, useful in practitioner work, not a diagnostic substitute.

**4. The conditions of daily labor.** The broader sixth-house meaning — work, daily routine, the conditions of the everyday — has direct medical relevance. The body that works under degrading conditions is constitutionally affected by those conditions. The sixth-house ruler's condition reports on the daily-conditions register the body labors under.

**5. The "house of servants" — daily dependency.** The modern translation of "servants" as daily-life dependency relationships preserves the classical doctrine's recognition that the body's daily care is partly mediated through others — and the conditions of those relationships matter constitutionally.

**Conditions traditionally noted from sixth-house emphasis:** the full range of medical conditions, with the sixth-house lord's sign and aspects determining the symbolic character. Specific traditional readings exist for sixth-house Saturn (chronic, melancholic conditions), sixth-house Mars (acute, hot, injury-coded conditions), and so on. Observatory preserves these in Workbench mode and translates them for user-facing surfaces.

## Mixed testimony

**Cadency and the sixth house.** The doctrine that the sixth house is structurally weak (cadent) carries the implicit suggestion that "illness placements are weak." This is technically true (the sixth has lower dignity weight than angular houses) and can land badly when applied to people whose sixth-house placements are constitutionally significant. The reframing: cadency means **expression-context-dependent**, not "weak in importance." A sixth-house Saturn is constitutionally important; the cadency means its expression is most visible in daily-conditions-register rather than in angular self-presentation.

**Sixth house in horary vs. decumbiture vs. natal.**
- In **natal** charts, the sixth house describes the constitutional pattern of daily-conditions, work, and illness register over the lifespan.
- In **horary** charts (questions about an illness), the sixth house represents the disease itself for the question being asked.
- In **decumbiture** charts (the chart of taking-to-bed at illness onset), the sixth house represents the disease taking hold at that specific moment.

The three readings overlap but are not identical. Observatory distinguishes them in Workbench mode.

**Prognostic content.** Older sources (notably Lilly, Saunders, the broader 17th-century English tradition) extract extensive prognostic content from sixth-house reading — recovery timelines, severity assessments, "signs of life or death" (the phrase appears explicitly in Saunders). **Observatory excludes the prognostic content entirely.** The doctrine is documented in Part VII for completeness; the modules never use it.

**Mental illness attribution.** Some traditional sources extend sixth-house reading to mental illness when combined with Mercury or Moon afflictions. Observatory uses such attributions with extreme caution and the firewall held strictly — chart placements never diagnose mental health conditions.

**Modern psychological extensions.** Modern psychological astrology has reframed the sixth house as "work patterns," "service-orientation," "perfectionism," and "health-as-practice" in ways that move the classical medical content into a softer, more lifestyle-oriented register. Observatory uses such reframings carefully — the classical medical content is the original doctrine; the psychological overlays are useful for user-facing language but should not displace the technical reading.

## Observatory translation

The sixth house describes the **daily-conditions field** — the answer to *what does this body labor under, day in and day out?*

> **Traditional:** The sixth house signifies diseases, illness, daily work, servants, and the body's daily conditions; the sixth-house ruler is read for the disease itself in horary/decumbiture work and for daily-conditions patterns in natal work.
>
> **Observatory:** The sixth-house ruler is read for the **daily-conditions register** the body labors under — the work, the routine, the small dependencies, the chronic patterns of how the everyday is lived. Used in Natal Blueprint as the daily-life anchor; in Body Weather, transits through the sixth house carry "daily routine engagement" flavor. Prognostic content from the classical tradition is documented in Part VII and **never used in any module**.

### How Observatory uses it

**In Natal Blueprint:**
- Sixth-house ruler's sign and placement → the register and field of the body's daily-conditions themes.
- Planets in the sixth → contributions to daily routine, work patterns, the body's everyday weather, and chronic-pattern register.
- **Translation pattern:** sixth-house placements are translated from "disease indicator" to "daily-conditions register" — what the body habitually engages with, not what will go wrong with it.

**In Body Weather:**
- Transits through the sixth house highlight the daily-routine field. A Saturn transit here is "review the structural conditions of your daily life" weather; a Jupiter transit, "expand the daily care register" weather.
- These are framed as **constitutional opportunities to attend to the daily**, not as illness alerts.

**In Workbench:**
- The full classical sixth-house reading is available — sixth-house ruler condition, dignity, aspects, derived placements — for the practitioner who wants the technical depth.
- Translation work is foregrounded: each technical reading is paired with its Observatory translation to ensure the user-facing surface stays within the safety floor.
- **Prognostic content** (recovery timelines, severity assessments, life/death signs) is explicitly **excluded** from this view.

**In Decumbiture:**
- The decumbiture sixth-house is the **disease itself at onset** — its symbolic character, its body-region register, its planetary signature.
- Read as **symbolic only** — what the disease is symbolically like, what register it sits in, what supportive practices might be resonant. Never as diagnosis, never as prognosis, never as treatment.

## When sixth-house emphasis is a resource

A well-emphasized sixth house — strong lord, supportive aspects, well-dignified planets — gives **daily-conditions fluency**:

- The body knows its routines and how to honor them.
- Work patterns are sustainable; the body is supported by, rather than depleted by, its labor.
- Daily care is constitutional — the small practices that maintain the body across decades are reliable and steady.
- The relationships of daily dependency (the partner who shares cooking, the colleague whose work supports yours) are healthy.

This is a **profoundly underrated** constitutional resource. Many chronic conditions are managed primarily through daily-conditions work; the body that has fluency here is constitutionally advantaged.

## Counterbalance — when sixth-house emphasis is over-running

When the sixth house is heavily emphasized and the daily-conditions register has become the body's primary axis:

- The body becomes entirely subsumed in routine; the angular vitality (first house) and the broader life-fields (seventh, tenth) are crowded out.
- Service-orientation becomes self-erasure; the body labors for others without daily-conditions support of its own.
- Perfectionist routine becomes a constitutional cage; flexibility is lost.
- Illness-as-identity can emerge — the body that is always managing conditions can lose access to the register of *not being ill*.

Counterbalance is the **twelfth house** (in the cadent-axis pairing — sixth and twelfth are both cadent, both "out of sight" in different ways). The body that always works in daily routine benefits from the practice of rest, retreat, and the unstructured time the twelfth house holds.

But more dynamically, counterbalance is also offered by the **first house** (vitality, baseline self), the **fifth** (pleasure and recreation), and the **tenth** (public role beyond daily labor). The sixth-house body needs the other houses' fields to remember it has them.

## Cross-tradition note (TCM)

The sixth house's intestine inheritance from Virgo maps onto **TCM Spleen and Stomach (the Earth-element pair)** in the digestive register, and onto **TCM Large Intestine (Metal)** in the eliminative register. The daily-routine emphasis maps onto TCM's broader principle of **regularity** — regular meals, regular sleep, regular bowel movements, regular work-and-rest cycles — as foundational to constitutional health.

Where the systems converge: both traditions treat **the daily as constitutionally primary**. TCM places enormous weight on the regularity of daily life as preventive medicine; the Western sixth-house emphasis carries the same principle through the symbolic register.

Where they diverge: TCM treats illness as **pattern-of-disharmony** to be addressed by tracking the body's signs (pulse, tongue, complexion); the Western sixth-house tradition treats illness as **symbolic field** to be interpreted through chart dynamics. The two are complementary; TCM works on the body directly, the Western sixth-house reading works on the symbolic context. Together they can support each other.

**Particularly relevant TCM pattern:** the "Spleen qi deficiency" pattern in TCM (low energy, sluggish digestion, easy fatigue, foggy thinking, weak limbs) has a strong felt-overlap with afflicted sixth-house presentations — particularly when Virgo or Saturn are also engaged in the sixth. The TCM remedy (regular warm meals, moderate movement, attention to digestive fire, avoidance of cold and damp) is directly applicable as supportive practice.

## Safety flags ⚠

- **Disease diagnosis firewall.** The sixth-house ruler, its condition, and its aspects do **not** diagnose specific illnesses. "Sixth-house Saturn afflicted by Mars" is symbolic language about constitutional register, never a clinical diagnosis. Even when the symbolic reading is suggestive (e.g., chronic-and-cold conditions resonant with the placement), Observatory does not name a clinical condition.
- **Prognosis firewall — strict.** The classical sixth-house tradition contains extensive prognostic doctrine — recovery timelines, severity grading, life/death signs (especially in decumbiture work). **Observatory excludes all of this from every module.** This content is documented in Part VII for completeness; it never reaches a user.
- **Mental illness firewall.** Sixth-house placements (particularly with Mercury or Moon afflictions) have classical attributions to mental conditions. **Observatory does not interpret mental health from chart placements** — neither as constitutional disposition nor as risk.
- **Chronic illness identity firewall.** People living with chronic conditions sometimes receive astrological readings that pathologize their constitution or frame their condition as "in the chart." Observatory rejects this entirely — chronic illness is medical and lived experience, never astrologically caused or astrologically predicted.
- **Work-as-pathology firewall.** The "service-orientation" and "perfectionism" modern psychological framings of the sixth house can become pathologizing. Observatory uses these descriptively, not diagnostically.
- **Servant/employee language.** The classical "house of servants" language is preserved in technical documentation but **never used in user copy** — modern translation is to "daily dependency relationships" or "the people whose daily work supports yours."
- **Pet-loss prediction firewall.** The "small animals" attribution does not generate predictions about pet illness or loss.
- **Sixth-house translation rule (mandatory).** Every sixth-house technical reading that reaches a user-facing surface must be paired with its Observatory translation. The raw doctrine "your sixth-house Saturn shows chronic Saturnine disease" is **never** delivered to a user; the translation "your sixth-house Saturn shows a constitutional register of structured daily-conditions work and a benefit from slow, durable rhythms" is.
