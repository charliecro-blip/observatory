# Part II — Planetary Combinations

Medical astrology rarely operates on single planets in isolation. The body is described by *how planets combine* — by aspect, by mutual reception, by clusters in a single house or sign, by midpoints between two planets, and by larger configurations (T-squares, grand trines, yods). A single planet's signature is real; *what that signature does* depends almost entirely on *who it's with*.

This file outlines the major combinatorial structures, common planetary pairs in the medical register, and Observatory's stance on how each is surfaced to users.

## 11.1 Aspects — the basic combinatorial vocabulary

Aspects are *angular relationships* between planets. The major aspects:

| Aspect | Angle | Classical character | Medical reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conjunction** | 0° | Blending — the two planets act as one | The qualities merge; whichever is stronger by sign and dignity dominates |
| **Opposition** | 180° | Tension across the axis — visibility, polarization | The two planets pull in opposite directions; the body experiences the *contrast* between them as friction |
| **Square** | 90° | Friction, action under pressure | The qualities clash; the body must work to integrate them; often a driver of symptomatic expression |
| **Trine** | 120° | Flow, ease, support | The qualities cooperate; the body can express both without strain |
| **Sextile** | 60° | Opportunity, gentle support | Cooperation that requires use; the support is available but not automatic |
| **Quincunx (inconjunct)** | 150° | Awkward, asymmetrical, requiring adjustment | The qualities don't naturally relate; the body has to *invent* the bridge; modern medical astrology often associates quincunxes with chronic patterns that "don't add up" |

A few medical-astrology notes:

- **Hard aspects** (square, opposition, conjunction-with-malefic) are classically read as *disease-producing*. Observatory retires this framing. Hard aspects produce *friction* — the body has to do work to integrate the qualities. The work itself can be symptomatic, but the framing is *demanding combination*, not *disease verdict*.
- **Soft aspects** (trine, sextile) are classically read as *fortunate*. They describe combinations the body can express without strain. They are not *better* in any moral sense; sometimes a body needs the friction of a hard aspect to *develop* a capacity.
- **Orbs** — aspects are not exact; they operate within a few degrees of the exact angle. Classical orbs are wider (8–10°); modern (especially Ebertin-influenced) work uses tighter orbs (3–5° for major aspects, 1° for midpoints). Observatory's Workbench uses *moderate orbs* (5–7° for major aspects) for natal work, *tight orbs* (1–3°) for transit work.
- **Applying vs. separating** — an aspect *applying* (the faster planet moving toward the slower) is read as *building, increasing energy*; one *separating* is *releasing, decreasing energy*. Useful in decumbiture for tempo reading.

## 11.2 Common planetary pairs in the medical register

The combinations below are the ones that recur in classical and modern medical-astrology literature. Each pair is described in terms of *what the combination signifies as body theme*, with safety flags where appropriate.

### Sun – Moon (the luminaries)

The body's *vital fire and lived flesh* together. The Sun-Moon relationship in a chart is the **fundamental constitutional axis**: how the body's active and receptive principles relate.

- **Conjunction** (new moon birth): vitality and rhythm fused; often a body whose Solar self and Lunar self are *closely woven*, sometimes hard to distinguish *what I want* from *what I feel*.
- **Opposition** (full moon birth): vitality and rhythm in *visibility-to-each-other*; the body can clearly see both registers but may experience them as in tension.
- **Square** (first/last quarter): productive friction between active and receptive; the body that has to *work* to integrate its Solar drive with its Lunar needs.
- **Trine/sextile**: cooperation between the two principles; supports a body that lives both registers comfortably.

The Sun-Moon relationship is checked early in every chart for *constitutional balance*. A heavily Solar chart with a debilitated Moon reads as a body running on vital fire without sufficient receptive rest; a heavily Lunar chart with a debilitated Sun reads as a body running on flow and feeling without sufficient self-coherence.

### Sun – Mars

**Vital fire + active heat.** A combination that *amplifies* the body's heat and assertive register.

- **Resource side:** robust vital tone, strong assertion, physical courage, capacity for vigorous work.
- **Stressed side:** cardiac and inflammatory signatures, hypertensive patterns, burnout from over-driving, anger that takes a cardiac toll.
- ⚠ **Safety:** the Sun-Mars combination is one of the cardiac signatures in modern medical astrology. The cardiac-symptom severity escalation applies absolutely. The user is never told their chart predicts cardiac events.

### Sun – Saturn

**Vital fire + limit / structure.** The body's animation working *within* a structural constraint.

- **Resource side:** dignity, endurance, the capacity for sustained effort, vital tone that holds up over time, the gravitas of a body that knows itself.
- **Stressed side:** chronic vitality patterns, depression-adjacent registers, the body whose vital fire is *suppressed by structure* (too much obligation, too little permission to shine), aging-themes prominent earlier than typical.
- ⚠ **Safety:** Sun-Saturn aspects are sometimes read as *vitality limitation, depression-prone constitution, chronic illness*. Observatory describes *signature*, not condition. Mental-health symptom escalation applies.

### Moon – Mars

**Rhythm + heat.** The body's flow energized or agitated by the active register.

- **Resource side:** energetic emotional life, capacity for action that is grounded in feeling, vigorous instinctual response.
- **Stressed side:** menstrual conditions (in some readings — the heat in the rhythmic register), gastric heat (stomach inflammation patterns), emotional reactivity, anger erupting through emotional channels, sleep disturbance from agitation.
- ⚠ **Safety:** Moon-Mars is sometimes invoked for menstrual/reproductive condition claims. Reproductive-condition firewall applies. The *shape* of "rhythmic heat" is what's named; specific clinical conditions are medical territory.

### Moon – Saturn

**Rhythm + limit.** The body's flow contained, structured, or restricted.

- **Resource side:** emotional steadiness, the capacity to hold one's feelings without flooding, mature self-soothing, the body's containment of its own rhythms.
- **Stressed side:** **the classical signature of melancholic depression** in the medical-astrology literature. Emotional contraction, the felt-sense of being held in by something heavier than oneself, sleep disturbance from rumination, the body cold and dry on the Lunar side (lunar reserves spent or unavailable).
- ⚠ **Safety:** Moon-Saturn is *the* most-named depressive signature in modern medical astrology. ⚠ Observatory does not diagnose depression from chart factors. The signature is *real* in the descriptive sense — many bodies with Moon-Saturn aspects do experience this register; many do not. User reports indicating clinical depression trigger safety floor escalation absolutely. The user is *never* told their chart explains, predicts, or causes depression.

### Mars – Saturn

**Heat + cold; activity + limit.** A combination that the tradition treats as particularly difficult — fast-and-hot meeting slow-and-cold.

- **Resource side:** the body's capacity for *disciplined effort* — the soldier's body, the marathoner's body, the body that can push hard *and* pace itself. This is real and substantial.
- **Stressed side:** the body in *cross-current* — wanting to act while being held back, wanting to rest while being driven, the "freeze" response (in modern trauma framing — Mars and Saturn both active in different directions). Chronic injury patterns. The body that has been pushing hard against limit for a long time.
- **Safety:** Mars-Saturn is sometimes read as the "accident" signature in older modern astrology. Observatory does not predict accidents. The "freeze response" framing is *signature*, not trauma diagnosis.

### Jupiter – Saturn

**Expansion + contraction.** The two outermost classical planets — the *great cycle* of the tradition (their conjunction marks a roughly 20-year cycle).

- **Resource side:** balance between growth and structure, the capacity to *build sustainably* (Jupiter's vision tempered by Saturn's discipline). The body that *lasts* because it both nourishes itself and holds shape.
- **Stressed side:** the body caught between *expanding* and *contracting* — wanting to grow while being held in, oscillating between abundance and lack. Plethoric-then-depleted patterns. The body that *binges and restricts* (whether food, work, anything).
- **The great cycle:** Jupiter-Saturn conjunctions every ~20 years are sometimes felt as *constitutional reset moments* — supportive practice framing for these is *integration of expansion and structure*.

### Venus – Mars

**Cool wet + hot dry.** The classical *temperature-balance* combination. Also the classical pair for erotic and relational themes.

- **Resource side:** balance between *pleasure-receiving* and *active-doing*. The body that can both *enjoy* and *act*. Sexual integration in the classical sense. Affectionate vigor.
- **Stressed side:** the body torn between comfort and action (wanting both, accomplishing neither), oscillation between indulgence and over-exertion.
- **Safety:** The relationship-prediction firewall applies. The sexual register handled matter-of-factly. Observatory's medical surface stays out of relationship analysis.

### Mercury – Mars

**Signal + sharp.** The body's nervous system and information work meeting active heat.

- **Resource side:** sharp thinking, fast articulation, mental energy, the capacity for verbal confrontation, "thinking on one's feet."
- **Stressed side:** irritable speech, mental burnout, "anger expressed through words," nervous-system agitation, jaw and shoulder tension from holding sharp words, "accident-prone communication" in the older literature.
- **Safety:** ⚠ The "accident-prone" framing is retired. Mercury-Mars is *signature*, not predictor.

### Mercury – Saturn

**Signal + slow / limit.** The body's nervous system and information work meeting structural limit.

- **Resource side:** deep, sustained thinking; the capacity for long focus; serious scholarship; precision in speech.
- **Stressed side:** **the classical signature of melancholic cognition** — slow speech under depression, rumination, the body's information work running cold and dry, the felt-sense of one's mind being "stuck." Cognitive depression-adjacent patterns.
- **Safety:** ⚠ As with Moon-Saturn, this is a named depressive signature in modern medical astrology. The same firewall applies: signature, not diagnosis. Safety floor escalation for clinical depression patterns.

### Mercury – Uranus

**Signal + electric.** Fast-signaling meeting the spike-discharge register.

- **Resource side:** fast intuitive thinking, breakthrough insights, neurological flexibility, the capacity to make unexpected connections, often *creative or original cognition*.
- **Stressed side:** scattered thinking, racing thought, nervous-system over-activation, the body unable to settle the mind, sleep disturbance from electrical mental activity.
- **Safety:** ⚠ The neurodivergence firewall applies absolutely. Mercury-Uranus is the most commonly invoked aspect for "ADHD" in popular astrology. Observatory does **not** characterize, diagnose, or pathologize neurodivergence from chart factors. Mercury-Uranus describes a *style of cognition*, often valuable, not a *disorder*.

### Mercury – Neptune

**Signal + diffuse.** The body's nervous system and information work meeting permeability and dissolution.

- **Resource side:** intuitive thinking, associative cognition, dream-rich mental life, the capacity to perceive subtle patterns, often *artistic or contemplative cognition*.
- **Stressed side:** mental fog, difficulty focusing, the body's information work running through too much *noise*, confusion, susceptibility to misinformation or self-deception, sleep disturbance from un-settled mental drift.
- **Safety:** ⚠ Observatory does not diagnose cognitive impairment or dissociative-spectrum conditions from this aspect. Signature, not condition.

### Sun – Pluto

**Vital fire + deep transformation.** The body's central animation meeting the underground register.

- **Resource side:** *the capacity to survive and rebuild*. Bodies with Sun-Pluto aspects often carry profound vital resources for survival, regeneration, and deep transformation. The Pluto resource framing applies with extra weight.
- **Stressed side:** the body's vital fire *darkened* by what it has been through; the felt-sense of having to *hide one's own light*; the survivor's energy that can be hard to share. Sometimes: the body's vital expression *transformed* by major life events.
- **Safety:** ⚠ Cancer attribution firewall applies absolutely. The "transformation" framing in user surfaces is *deep restructuring*, never *crisis* or *death-and-rebirth* in the literal register.

### Moon – Pluto

**Rhythm + deep transformation.** The body's lived flow meeting underground intensity.

- **Resource side:** deep emotional life, the capacity to feel and hold profound feeling, emotional regenerative work, the body's relationship with its own depths.
- **Stressed side:** emotional intensity that can flood the body, the felt-sense of being unable to fully release certain emotional patterns, attachment intensity (in modern psychological framing — "Moon-Pluto and the mother," a problematic but persistent attribution).
- **Safety:** ⚠ The "maternal trauma" framing widely associated with Moon-Pluto in modern psychological astrology is **firewalled** from Observatory's medical surface. Family-of-origin and trauma diagnoses are not chart-derived. Mood-disorder escalation applies.

### Venus – Saturn

**Pleasure + limit.** The body's pleasure-receiving register meeting structural constraint.

- **Resource side:** mature pleasure, the dignity of small enjoyments faithfully kept, beauty that endures, fidelity, the body's capacity for *committed* pleasure.
- **Stressed side:** pleasure-deficit patterns, the felt-sense that one *doesn't deserve* enjoyment, hardness around softness, the body that holds itself back from receiving.
- **Safety:** Body-image firewall applies. Relationship-prediction firewall applies.

### Venus – Uranus

**Pleasure + electric.** The body's pleasure register meeting the unexpected.

- **Resource side:** spontaneous pleasure, unusual aesthetic life, sudden love, breaking out of pleasure-suppression patterns.
- **Stressed side:** unstable pleasure-seeking, the body's pleasure register hard to settle, sudden withdrawal of comfort.

### Venus – Neptune

**Pleasure + diffuse.** The body's pleasure register meeting permeability.

- **Resource side:** aesthetic depth, the body's response to beauty in its dissolving register, compassionate love.
- **Stressed side:** ⚠ this is one of the modern aspect-readings for *substance use, addiction-vulnerable pleasure patterns, escape through pleasure*. Observatory does not diagnose addiction; the signature is *signature*, not condition.

### Mars – Pluto

**Heat + deep.** The body's active register meeting the underground.

- **Resource side:** the body's capacity for *deep, sustained, profound work* — long projects, deep recovery, deep eliminative work. Sexual depth (the Mars-Pluto-Scorpio signature). Survival in active form.
- **Stressed side:** the body's active register *intensified beyond settle-ability*, sustained fight-or-flight, controlled aggression that doesn't release.
- **Safety:** Sexual content matter-of-fact. Violence-prediction firewall applies absolutely.

## 11.3 Midpoints (Ebertin-style)

Reinhold Ebertin's *Combination of Stellar Influences* developed a systematic midpoint method: the midpoint of two planets is the *halfway angular point* between them, and a third planet falling at or near that midpoint *integrates* the two. The midpoint Sun/Saturn = the *integration point* of vital fire and structural limit; a transiting Mars conjuncting this midpoint *activates* the integration.

Midpoint work is part of the Workbench's full chart-analysis toolkit but is generally not surfaced to users (the abstraction is high). Key principles:

- **Tight orbs.** Midpoint work uses very small orbs (often 1° or less for natal, 30' for transit).
- **Medical midpoints recur.** The Sun/Saturn (vitality structure), Sun/Mars (cardiac/inflammatory), Moon/Saturn (depressive register), and Mars/Saturn (chronic action-limit) midpoints recur as recognized medical signatures.
- ⚠ **The same firewalls apply.** A midpoint signature is *signature*, not condition. Disease-name translation protocol applies.

## 11.4 Larger configurations

Modern medical astrology recognizes several *multi-planet patterns*:

- **T-square** — three planets in a square+opposition configuration. Read as a *focused tension* — the body has a particular nexus of friction-work to integrate.
- **Grand trine** — three planets in mutual trine. Read as a *closed loop of ease*, sometimes critiqued as a register the body doesn't *have to work* to maintain (so doesn't develop strength from).
- **Yod ("Finger of God")** — two planets in sextile, both quincunx to a third. Read as an *unusual asymmetrical pattern*, often invoked for chronic or hard-to-resolve patterns.
- **Stellium** — three or more planets in one sign or house. Read as a *concentrated emphasis* — the body's life heavily organized around that sign's qualities or house's life arena.
- **Kite** — a grand trine with a fourth planet opposite one of the three. Reads as a *grand trine activated by tension*.

Each of these is part of the Workbench's chart-synthesis toolkit. User surfaces use these configurations *very lightly* — naming "you have a Cancer stellium" is informative; turning it into a *constitutional verdict* is not.

## 11.5 Mutual reception

When two planets are each in the *other's* sign (e.g. Sun in Cancer, Moon in Leo — the Sun is in the Moon's domicile and vice versa), they are in **mutual reception**. The classical reading: each planet *helps the other*, lending its strength. Mutual reception can rescue a debilitated planet.

In medical-astrology terms, mutual reception is a *resource flag*: a body whose key planets are in mutual reception has internal supportive structures that *cross-strengthen*. Observatory's Workbench surfaces mutual receptions; user copy often doesn't need to name them explicitly.

## 11.6 Observatory's stance on combination work

Three principles for how the app surfaces combinations:

1. **Single-planet readings are rarely complete.** Body Weather and Natal Blueprint output that names *one planet* without context is usually impoverished. The combinations matter as much as the planets.
2. **Combination doctrine carries the same safety firewalls as single-planet doctrine.** The Moon-Saturn depressive signature, Sun-Mars cardiac signature, Mercury-Uranus neurodivergence framing, Sun-Pluto cancer-attribution risk, Mars-Saturn freeze-and-trauma framing — all carry their respective firewalls. Combination *amplifies* the signal; it does not change the rules.
3. **The Workbench can handle complexity; user surfaces should be cautious.** A practitioner can read a Sun-Mars-Saturn T-square with full context and produce useful work for their client. A user-facing surface that names the same configuration risks producing fear or confusion. The Workbench is the place for full combinatorial work; user surfaces describe the *felt-sense weather* without always naming the structural cause.
