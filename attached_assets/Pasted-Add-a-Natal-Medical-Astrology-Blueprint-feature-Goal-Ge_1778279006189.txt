Important:
Add a specific “Contradictions and Mixed Testimony” section.

This section should handle cases like:
- the same planet rules both 6th and 10th houses
- a planet is both supportive and challenging
- a house ruler has dignity but difficult aspects
- a benefic rules a difficult house
- a malefic is well dignified or constructively placed
- the chart shows both heat and cold, dryness and moisture, activation and depletion

The report should not flatten contradictions.
It should say things like:
“Jupiter ruling both the 6th and 10th links illness/routine themes with therapeutic direction. This can suggest that the same principle that destabilizes the system may also become part of the remedy, depending on how it is cultivated.”
