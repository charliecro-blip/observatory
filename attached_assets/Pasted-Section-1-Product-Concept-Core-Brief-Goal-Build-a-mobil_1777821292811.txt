Section 1: Product Concept / Core Brief

Goal: Build a mobile/web app that functions as an astrology-informed health pattern journal.

Core promise:

Track your body. Understand your patterns. Time your life better.

The app helps users understand relationships between:

sleep
energy
mood
digestion
symptoms
stress
behavior
calendar load
astrology/transits
lunar cycles

The app should not diagnose or prescribe. It should give low-risk lifestyle reflections and pattern-based suggestions.

Primary user question:

“Why might I feel this way today, and what should I do with my energy?”

Section 2: MVP User Flow

Build the app around this daily loop:

User opens app.
App shows a simple Today dashboard.
User completes a quick daily check-in.
App stores the check-in.
App combines check-in data with astrology data.
App gives a short daily interpretation.
User can later review patterns over time.

The first version does not need full wearable integration, calendar integration, or advanced AI. Start with manual tracking + basic astrology + saved history.

Section 7: Insight Engine

The app should generate insights from three layers:

1. User-reported data

Example:

“You reported low energy, poor sleep, and high stress today.”

2. Astrology context

Example:

“The Moon is in a more mentally active sign today, which may correspond with increased restlessness or overthinking.”

3. Practical suggestion

Example:

“Choose short focused tasks, reduce stimulation tonight, and avoid making major emotional decisions while tired.”

The insight should be clearly framed as reflection, not diagnosis.

Section 8: “Why Might I Feel This Way?” Feature

This should be the app’s central feature.

Create a button on the Today page:

Why might I feel this way?

When tapped, the app reviews:

today’s check-in
recent check-ins
symptom tags
behavior tags
current Moon phase/sign
current transits
recent stress or sleep changes

Then it generates a response:

Example:

You may be feeling this way because your sleep quality was low, stress is above your usual baseline, and you logged social overload yesterday. Today’s astrology also emphasizes mental activity and nervous-system stimulation. This is probably a better day for simplifying than pushing harder.

Section 14: Safety Rules

The app must include health safety boundaries.

The AI should not:

diagnose illness
prescribe herbs, supplements, or medications
tell users to stop medication
claim astrology causes medical conditions
give emergency medical advice as a substitute for care
make deterministic claims

The AI can:

reflect patterns
suggest low-risk lifestyle supports
recommend rest, hydration, journaling, movement, sunlight, sleep hygiene
suggest seeking professional care for serious symptoms
encourage users to contact a clinician when symptoms are severe, persistent, or concerning

Include a red-flag disclaimer:

This app is for reflection, self-tracking, and personal insight. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace medical care. Seek urgent medical help for severe chest pain, fainting, sudden weakness, severe shortness of breath, suicidal thoughts, or rapidly worsening symptoms.

Section 17: Second Replit Prompt After Prototype

Once the basic app exists, give Replit this next:

Now add a simple daily insight generator.

When the user saves a daily check-in, generate a body weather summary based on their scores and tags.

Use this logic:

- If stress is 7 or above, mention nervous system load.
- If digestion is 4 or below, mention digestive sensitivity.
- If energy is 4 or below, set capacity to Low.
- If energy is 5–7, set capacity to Medium.
- If energy is 8 or above and stress is below 6, set capacity to High.
- If anxiety, restlessness, or brain fog are selected, suggest reducing stimulation.
- If fatigue or insomnia are selected, suggest rest and earlier bedtime.
- If conflict or overwork are selected, suggest boundaries and decompression.

Generate:
1. Capacity level
2. Body weather summary
3. Best use of the day
4. Watch-for themes
5. Supportive suggestions

Keep all language non-medical, practical, and reflective.
