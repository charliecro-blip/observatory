Improve the quality-control rules for Body Weather and Oracle medical-astrology language.

Current issue:
Some generated sentences are grammatically awkward or too vague, for example:
“With Cancer rising, your body thrives on warm, nourishing foods prepared at home are foundational to your health.”

We need clearer, more specific, more nuanced prose.

Update the AI system prompts and/or post-processing instructions so outputs follow these rules:

1. Grammar and clarity
- Use complete, grammatically correct sentences.
- Avoid awkward compound sentences.
- Avoid phrases like “purpose vitality is active” or “vitality is active” unless clearly explained.
- If a phrase sounds like a keyword dump, rewrite it into natural language.

2. Specificity
Avoid generic phrases like:
- “your vitality is active”
- “this is a time of transformation”
- “pay attention to your body”
- “balance is needed”

Instead, explain:
- what part of the chart/transit is being referenced
- what body-weather domain it may correspond with
- what the user should track
- what kind of support may be relevant

3. Medical astrology safety
Do not diagnose.
Do not prescribe.
Do not say astrology causes symptoms.
Use:
- “may correspond with”
- “is worth tracking”
- “can symbolize”
- “may describe a tendency”
- “in your logs, this appears alongside…”

4. Body Weather structure
Every interpretation should separate:
- Observed check-in data
- Natal chart context
- Transit context
- Practical tracking suggestion
- Supportive reflection

5. No vague transit statements
If mentioning a transit, include:
- the transiting planet
- the natal point or house involved
- whether it is mild/moderate/strong
- why it matters
- what domain to track

Example:
Instead of:
“Neptune square your natal Moon — vitality is active.”

Say:
“Neptune is currently forming a square to your natal Moon. This is a stronger transit signature for sensitivity, porousness, dreaminess, confusion, or diffuse emotional/body signals. Since the Moon is central to bodily rhythm and regulation, it may be worth tracking sleep, hydration, emotional overwhelm, and sensitivity to food or environment during this period.”

6. Edit for readability
Outputs should sound like a thoughtful practitioner, not a horoscope generator or clinical report.
