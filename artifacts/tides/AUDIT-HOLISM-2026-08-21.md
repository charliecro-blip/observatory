# Holism audit — is this becoming a strained astro-productivity app?

2026-08-21, late. Owner: "I'm unsure we thoroughly thought through the
productivity styles conversation… I especially want this to feel holistic,
and not entirely a productivity app, but also something that encourages
people to make space for more yin activities — moon and venus type stuff,
or even a bit of saturn."

Measured against the build as it stands tonight, not against the intent.

## 1. Where we are, measured

**The engine is holistic. The planning and record layers are work-shaped.
The new rhythm layer inherited the planning layer's shape.**

So the strain is real and locatable. It is not the astrology and not the
hour-level copy; it is that **the three surfaces a person touches most —
Home's list, the shaped day, the evening harvest — only know how to hold
doings**, and the rhythm presets, built on those surfaces, inherited it.
"One move" can only be a task. "Three ways in" are three tasks. The
harvest counts wins.

## 2. The frame to hold it

Not a separate yin mode (that would be a second app inside the first),
and not a softer skin. **Everything the app places, counts or leads with
gets a second kind of item it can hold: a keeping.** Doings are done;
keepings are kept — rest, pleasure, company, the practice, the stop.
The ledger already has the verb ("kept: Wind down"); the rest of the app
does not.

The three planets the owner named are the three keepings:

- **Moon — tending.** Care for the body, the home, the people; rest that
  restores. The app's Moon-hour copy already says this; nothing lets you
  plan it or count it.
- **Venus — pleasure and company.** The date, the meal, the beautified
  room, the call. Venus hours suggest it; nothing places it.
- **Saturn — enough.** The stop, the boundary, the wind-down, the thing
  declined on purpose. The weaver's load ceiling (60% of waking time)
  and its 30-minute wind-down already embody this and never say so.

## 3. What to weave in, in order

1. **The word "work" stops being the generic noun.** Five sign approach
   lines and the Saturn line say "work" where they mean the day or the
   doing ("work goes by feel…", "the work arrives when…"). Rewrite in the
   lexicon: one edit each, every surface follows. Home's panel "YOUR WORK"
   becomes "WHAT YOU'RE HOLDING" (it already holds habits and stars).
2. **The record counts keepings.** The wins ledger gains a second list
   beside it — kept: rested, enjoyed, tended, stopped — fed by habits of
   the keeping kind, diary entries tied to Moon/Venus/Saturn stars, and a
   one-tap "kept" door on the evening harvest ("what restored you?"
   beside "what moved?"). The Log's tally shape shows both columns.
3. **Shape Day places rest.** The day weaver takes habits the way the
   week weaver does, and places a keeping when the tide is low, the Moon
   is void, or the Moon/Venus hour falls in open time — as a proposed
   block the person can keep or drop, never a silent fill. Its refusal
   already exists ("open · nothing needed placing here"); this gives the
   open stretch something to be for.
4. **The presets hold keepings.** Field's three ways in: one is always a
   keeping when one exists (a practice, a Moon/Venus star, a rest).
   Campaign's one move may be a keeping when the tide is out. Route's
   routines already include Wind down. Tide is unchanged.
5. **Guiding Stars offer yin aims.** The creation form's examples include
   "rest properly", "see my people weekly", "leave work at six" — each
   diagnosed to Moon, Venus, Saturn by the existing associate.ts. The
   Bearings room already shows which stars speak which planet.
6. **Saturn says enough.** When the day's committed load passes the
   ceiling, or at the wind-down hour, Home says so in one line ("Enough
   for today; the rest is tomorrow's") instead of carrying the remainder
   silently. Auto-rollover becomes opt-in rather than default, and
   "overdue" reads "past its date".
7. **The evening loop gets a Moon question.** Beside the harvest: what
   restored you, what you let be. Ten words, one tap.

Each of these is small; together they change what the app is for. None
of them adds a surface.

## 4. The audit worth running

A **rest-day simulation**: take three personas who need the yin (Jess the
pacer, Imani the somatic coach, Maya the Co-Star refugee) through one day
they intend to do nothing in, and one day they intend to spend on a
person rather than a project. Log every moment the app pushes: a task
rolled over, an "overdue", a harvest with nothing to harvest, a "one move"
that can only be a task, a streak. That list is the punch list for §3.

## 5. The strategic question, not mine to answer

The memory calls the frame "enchanted productivity" and the pricing line
"free = today, paid = the rhythm". The rhythm is holistic by design (the
Tide preset IS the whole-day app), but "productivity" in the frame pulls
the marketing and the next features toward doings. If the answer to
tonight's question is "a life app that knows when to push", the frame's
noun should change before the next feature does — or, as the owner said,
the work-first shape is another iteration of the app, not this one.
