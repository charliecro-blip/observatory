How the reading is made
  1. Starswell reads each waking planetary hour from now through tomorrow night at its midpoint, so a card changes only when the hour turns.
  2. The Moon and real aspects lead, as Charlie ruled on August 22. Her sign and phase, her applying contacts, and planet-to-planet aspects within 2° are primary evidence, and so are the Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars, Jupiter or Saturn on the Ascendant or Midheaven. The planetary hour and the day ruler add color and never count toward agreement.
  3. A void Moon runs from her last aspect to a classical planet until she changes sign. Beginnings are held back, and the card describes the void in that sign, with Lilly’s four easy voids marked.
  4. A hard Moon contact to Saturn makes a heavier stretch for three hours either side of exact, and one to Neptune makes a hazy stretch.
  5. When the Moon’s next aspect is an easy one to Venus or Jupiter, the window is marked graced.
  6. A card needs two different kinds of primary evidence to agree. It describes how the hour feels and lists a few things that suit it, and an activity goes first when it has an extra reason of its own.
  7. The first card is now, the second is the next different weather after any state ends, and the third is an edge when one is running. Empty slots stay empty.
