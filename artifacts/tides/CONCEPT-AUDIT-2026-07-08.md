# Concept audit — the ideas a user actually meets (2026-07-08)

Suggestions only; nothing here has been acted on. Walked the journey surface by
surface, cataloguing every intellectual concept as presented, then judged it
through the persona set (Dan the zero-astrology contractor, Maya the curious
beginner, Luna the fluent astrologer, Rachel who needs one clear verdict, Bill
who reads everything).

## Part 1 — The inventory (journey order)

**Intro:** weather-for-time · four characters (Deep/Surge/Building/Clear) ·
level (high/low, rising/ebbing) · "make it yours."
**Today:** tide character + level + element + moon % · flavor undertones
(saturnine…) · ritual (Cast off / Log the day) · streaks · Today's Three ·
the energy wave + aspect markers · Resonant Now (3 voices) · Big Sky (pair
weather + takes) · Standing Conditions (retrogrades) · felt rating · Sky
Clock (personal angles) · Guiding Stars · "1st-house year ruled by Mercury" ·
week bars.
**Rail:** season · daylight/light-phase · moon phase/sign/VOC · aspects w/
orbs · day ruler ("good for") · hour ruler + archetype ("The Sovereign") ·
your transits.
**Calendar:** grid · planetary-hour ladder · lunar markers · "QUALITY — next
30 days" bars · Almanac events + Reference (six-rung primer).
**Aims:** Guiding Stars (element-tagged, anchors "rides Saturn through your
9th", horizons) · long-weather header (profection + chapters) · Tasks
(windowTypes: deep_work/creative/admin/…) · Habits (streaks, favored
elements, resonant ✦) — plus a separate "practices" system with its own scale
(resonant/supported/soften/protect).
**Log:** lookback · flavors ("a plutonian day") · felt history · reflect.

## Part 2 — Where it's inconsistent or heavy

**C1 · One 4-fold, four vocabularies.** The same wheel is called Deep/Surge/
Building/Clear (Today), water/fire/earth/air (Aims, Plan, Log chips),
feel/act/build/think (copy), and "lanes" (weaver). Beginners must learn two
pairings; surfaces flip between them arbitrarily (Log timeline says "Surge",
the planner chip says "fire").

**C2 · Five grading scales.** Tide level (coherence) · planner tiers (great/
will-do/against) · electional verdicts (Strong/Workable/Caution/Avoid) ·
practices (resonant/supported/soften/protect) · calendar quality bars
(0–7 score). Rachel needs ONE answer to "is now good for this?"; she
currently gets five dialects of it. (The retired header label was a sixth.)

**C3 · Three parallel typing systems for a task.** Element + planet +
windowType (a 10-item taxonomy: deep_work, recovery, launch…). windowType is
pure jargon leak — it appears in QuickCapture ("Best time: any"), Tasks, and
ScheduleSuggest, and duplicates what element+planet already encode.

**C4 · "Currents / season / chapter / year" name-splits.** The profection is
called "your 1st-house year," "your season," "long weather," and appears in
three places (dashboard card, Aims header, Star Base houses). "Chapter" means
both profection-year and outer-planet-through-house. "Currents" survives as a
card title, a header phrase, and legacy copy — naming three different views.

**C5 · Two habit-like systems.** Habits (streaks, elements) AND practices
(timing-scored) both surface on Today/rail. Users can't tell them apart.

**C6 · Multiple archetype nicknames per planet.** Saturn is "The Builder"
(core), "the need for structure" (subtitle), "saturnine" (flavor), and the
rail hour calls planets "The Warrior/The Sage." Rich, but four handles per
planet is three too many for a beginner; one archetype + one adjective would
still be plenty of voice.

**C7 · VOC has four names.** Void of course · slack water · between
statements · "Between-tides"/spirit. Pick one plain name (slack water is the
best of them — it's already the tide metaphor) and let "void of course"
appear once as the traditional term.

**C8 · The nesting ladder is implicit.** Hour ⊂ day ⊂ moon-sign ⊂ month ⊂
season ⊂ year ⊂ chapter is the app's actual intellectual spine — the rail
implies it top-to-bottom and the primer lists it, but it's never DRAWN as one
picture a user can hold.

**C9 · Favorability leftovers.** The calendar's "QUALITY — NEXT 30 DAYS"
strip and the tide-chart caption ("Surge water · mid water") still speak the
old score dialect the header chip just dropped.

## Part 3 — Suggestions (not acted on)

**S1 · Declare three official axes; fold everything into them.**
- **CHARACTER** — what kind of time (the 4-fold; see S2).
- **CHARGE** — how much water (level/trend, one phrase).
- **FIT** — does this action suit this time: the new three-step tier language
  ("a great time / this time will do / against the current") becomes the ONE
  grading scale app-wide — practices, habit ✦, suggestion ranking, quality
  bars all re-expressed in it. Electional keeps its four-step verdict (it
  answers a different question — the quality of a *beginning* — and needs
  "Avoid") but renames its middle steps to match the family.

**S2 · One register rule for the 4-fold.** Plain word leads, element word is
the bilingual layer everywhere: chips say "Deep," tooltips/bilingual say
"water." Never show a bare element word to a plain-mode user again (Log
chips, planner chips, star tags all currently do).

**S3 · Retire windowType from the UI.** The association layer already maps
phrases → element+planet; let it also pick the window internally. One typing
system visible: element (planet detail for the curious).

**S4 · One name each for the long cycles.** Suggestion: **"Your year"**
(profection, everywhere — never season/chapter), **"Chapters"** (outer planet
through a house, exclusively), and retire the word **"Currents"** entirely —
its three appearances become "Your year & chapters" / "Long weather."

**S5 · Merge practices into habits** (or hide practices until the distinction
earns its keep). One daily-doing concept.

**S6 · One archetype + one adjective per planet.** Canon table: Saturn = the
Builder / saturnine; Mars = the Warrior / martial; etc. All surfaces draw
from that table (rail hour, Star Base, literacy, planner rationales).

**S7 · Draw the ladder.** One diagram — seven nested rings or a vertical
depth gauge (hour at the surface, chapters at the seabed) — used in three
places: the Almanac reference spine, onboarding slide 3, and as the mental
map behind the rail. Bill gets his book; Maya gets the picture that makes
the app make sense; Dan can ignore it.

**S8 · Dashboard jargon pass.** The "Currents · the long season" card
currently leads with "☿ Your 1st-house year ruled by Mercury" — the most
jargon-dense line on the home page. Plain version: "Your year: a self &
body year — identity work runs downhill until March." Same data, no houses
or rulers until tapped.

**S9 · Tab-level condensation (if wanted).** The 6 tabs are conceptually
clean (now / when / what / what-happened / when-to-do / who-you-are). The
real condensing win isn't fewer tabs — it's fewer *concepts per tab* (S1–S8).
If a tab must go someday, Log's timeline could become Today's "history" mode
(the ritual card already bridges them); Star Base rename candidates remain
Cosmos / Chart / Within.

**Sequencing suggestion:** S2+S9's dashboard line and C9's leftovers are
copy-only (hours); S1/S3/S4 are half-day passes each; S7 is a design session.
Do S1 before beta if anything — five grading scales is the thing testers
will actually trip on.
