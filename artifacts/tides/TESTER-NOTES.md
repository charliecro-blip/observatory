# Welcome to the Compass beta 🌊

Compass is a weather report for time — it reads the sky and tells you what
kind of moment you're in, and what it's good for. You don't need to know any
astrology to use it.

## Getting started (2 minutes)

1. Open [compass.day](https://compass.day) and walk through the intro.
2. **Birth details are optional but recommended** — they unlock your personal
   timing (your long seasons, your transits). Don't know your birth time?
   Check the box; you'll get an honest chart from what's knowable instead of
   a made-up one.
3. **Save your account key** (Settings → Account). There are no passwords —
   the key is the *only* way to get your data onto another device or back
   after clearing your browser. Screenshot it now.

## A quick tour

- **Today** — the day's weather: what kind of energy is running and what it
  favors. At the bottom, rate how the day felt and jot a one-line log entry.
- **Calendar** — your week with a 30-day forecast strip across the top (tap
  a bar to jump to that day), plus your Log (every rating and journal line,
  stamped with that day's sky) — ahead and behind in one place.
- **Aims** — your Guiding Stars (long-term aims), tasks, and habits — with
  your long seasons shown up top.
- **Plan** — paste in a to-do list and let the Planner weave it into your
  week; or pick a good moment to begin something.
- **✦ Ask** (top bar) — ask "what should I do with this moment?"

You'll also find doors that open as you go — when a day has a strong flavor,
Today offers to introduce you to the planet behind it.

## Two things worth knowing

- **Google Calendar**: when connecting, Google shows a scary
  "unverified app" screen — this is expected for a small beta. Click
  **Advanced → Go to Compass (unsafe)** to continue. The app only ever
  *reads* your calendar, never writes to it.
- **The felt-rating is the point.** The app gets more honest the more you
  tell it how days actually felt — after ~2 weeks it starts showing which
  tides genuinely work for you. That's the experiment you're part of.
