## A. First-run experience

### A1. Intro slides rework — the "nested layers" slide is broken in a specific way
`INTRO_SLIDES[3]` ("The sky is nested rhythms") renders `<SpineGauge compact/>`,
and **compact mode strips the labels and period names** — a new user sees bare
stacked gauges with no words attached. The one slide about the app's deepest
concept is the one slide that doesn't explain itself.

Options, in rough order of preference:
- **(a) Teach it later, not here.** Cut the slide to one sentence + a simple
  static nesting visual (hour ∈ day ∈ month ∈ year as concentric arcs), and let
  the walkthrough (A2) point at the real SpineGauge on the dashboard where it
  has labels and live data. Concepts land better on real data than on abstract
  diagrams.
- (b) Build a purpose-made animated visual for the slide (rings settling into
  one another). Prettier, more work, still teaches on fake data.
- (c) Un-compact the gauge on the slide. Cheapest, but the full gauge is an
  instrument, not an illustration — likely too dense for slide 4 of 6.

Also in scope: slide count (6 is a lot pre-value; consider 4), and whether
slide 5 ("Plan with it") and 6 ("Make it yours") collapse into one.

### A2. Spotlight walkthrough — highlight the real dashboard, don't narrate a modal
What exists today: a "New here? How Compass works" banner → opens a **read-it
modal** (`Guide.tsx`), plus a persistent "Set your first Guiding Star" banner.
Both are tell-don't-show.

Build: a coach-marks system — dim the page, spotlight one real element at a
time with 1–2 lines each, Next/Skip, replayable from the guide. Proposed stops
(one per journey, deliberately few):
1. **The tide hero** — "this is the day's weather; conditions, not fate" (now)
2. **The ritual card** — "mornings you cast off, evenings you log" (now)
3. **On deck / your day** — "what you've placed; ✓ when it happened" (now)
4. **Plan tab** — "dump a list, it weaves it into good windows" (planning)
5. **Aims tab** — "set a Guiding Star; days that suit it get flagged" (stars)
6. **The density toggle** — "the full instrument panel is one tap away"

Technical shape: a `data-tour="..."` attribute per stop + a fixed-position
overlay that reads `getBoundingClientRect()` — no library needed. Mobile: same
stops, bottom-sheet copy. Fires once after onboarding completes; the two
banners collapse into it (the star banner survives as walkthrough stop 5's
tail: "tap here when you're ready").

### A3. Onboarding order sanity
name → birth → chronotype. Chronotype is the quiet workhorse (ritual timing,
re-homing waking hours, planner hours) but reads as an optional afterthought.
One line of value-pitch on that step ("this is how Compass knows your morning
from everyone else's"), not a redesign. Chartless path ("Show me today →")
must get the same walkthrough — verify it does.

## B. Simplification around the three journeys

### B1. Today, essential density, fresh account — still ~9 stacked things
Screenshot-verified this session: two banners + hero + ritual card + Guiding
Star CTA + ON DECK + Waves + MOMENTS AHEAD + "Show the full instrument panel",
beside a rail showing SEASON / MOON / MOON ASPECTS / THIS DAY / THIS HOUR (all
5 upcoming hours) / PLANETARY ASPECTS (9) / retrogrades / WAVES.

- The **rail at `astroDetail: medium` is an instrument panel**, not an
  essential. Proposal: at essential density, collapse the rail to Season line +
  Moon chip + This Hour only; everything else behind the existing toggle.
  (`railDense` currently keys off astroDetail=full — wire it to uiDensity too.)
- The **two banners → walkthrough** (A2) removes two of the nine things.
- **MOMENTS AHEAD vs Waves vs ON DECK** — three list-like blocks answering
  adjacent questions. Assess merging Moments into Waves.

### B2. The in-app element contradiction — same bug we fixed in email, still live in-app
Screenshot: hero reads "**Surge Tide** · Initiative, courage" + "**A fire
day** — carried by the Mars hour" while the rail chip beside it reads "Moon in
**Pisces · water** · favors meditate, pray, drift". Two adjacent, opposite
instructions. The email fix was "only one place names an element" — the app
needs its own ruling. Likely resolution: the hero owns the day's *current*
(synthesis element); the Moon chip gets a clarifying micro-label ("Moon's
mood") so they read as two layers, not a contradiction. Needs a decision, then
a small copy/label change — not a rewrite.

### B3. Planning journey naming
Plan tab sub-tabs: **Schedule / Break down / Begin**. "Begin" (electional —
pick a day to start something) is the differentiator and the least
discoverable word of the three. Candidates: "Pick a day", "Begin something".
Also verify capture → "Dump & schedule" → Planner seam gets a walkthrough
mention (it's the magic path).

### B4. Guiding Stars journey
- First-star creation: suggestion CTA exists; verify the create → diagnose
  (planet) → first-step flow is coherent for someone who has never seen it.
- Star presence on Today at essential: currently a banner. After A2, assess
  whether an active star's "today suits it" line earns a place on the
  essential Dashboard (it's journey #3 and mostly invisible at essential).
- GuidingStarsHub is 1,138 lines with goals/projects/milestones/tasks/habits/
  sessions all in one page — beta-OK, but flag first-visit overwhelm; the
  walkthrough stop should land on "one star, one next step", not the machinery.

### B5. Known small UX debts that touch the three journeys (triage: beta or after)
- Hover-only interactions pass (§3b): HelpBadge glossary, Calendar hover "+
  add" — matters on phones, beta users will be on phones. **Lean: beta.**
- Reviews available-not-compulsory (§3b) — **after**.
- ScheduleSuggest enrichment (27-batch #10) — verify current state, then call.
- Preference sync scoping (§3b) — **after** (single-device beta acceptable).
