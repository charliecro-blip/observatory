## The product, one line

Compass is a daily driver that answers "what should I do right now" with a
reason — and how much of that reason comes from the sky is a dial the person
owns, from none at all to the full instrument.

## Three doors, three pitches

The intake's real segmentation ("Just the guidance / A little sky / The full
chart") is the campaign structure. One product, three true stories:

1. **Secular** — *a calm daily driver that schedules honestly and never
   shames you.* Plain reasons ("Due Friday, and you're free until 2 PM"),
   a scheduler that respects your calendar, touches instead of streak
   guilt, and an app that will say "nothing stands out today" instead of
   inventing work. The astrology is genuinely optional — a running focus
   session turns it off by itself.
2. **Curious** — *your day, read like weather.* The tide, the moon, best
   windows, plain language throughout. The existing voice; still right.
3. **Fluent** — *a real instrument, not a horoscope.* The rail, evidence
   receipts, testimony panels, dignities — and the same refusal culture:
   described conditions, never promised outcomes.

## New messages the old kit doesn't carry

- **"Astrology optional. Honesty always."** True since the quiet lens; the
  single biggest new claim. (Never spell it "we hid the astrology" — the
  engine is identical at every level; only what's shown changes.)
- **The comeback promise.** No streak shame: cadence is scored against the
  rhythm you chose, missed days carry forward without accusation, partial
  work counts as touches. Competitors churn people at the first missed
  week; say out loud that we don't.
- **The record.** The Wake accrues — everything finished, kept, and named,
  stamped with the day it happened. "Free is today; paid is the rhythm"
  still holds, and the record is the rhythm's face.
- **Sprints.** Short pushes with hard ends, sometimes riding a real
  transit window ("while Mars runs with Jupiter"), sometimes self-chosen
  (a dopamine fast, a meditation week). Each notable span is a small,
  honest campaign beat — a spell for finishing things, never a cosmic
  ultimatum. A finished sprint is the shareable unit once the share card
  exists (BACKLOG).

## What never to say (unchanged, plus)

Everything in the old §3, and: never sell the quiet lens as astrology
removed (it's a lens, not a fork); never gamify sprints into challenges
with losers; never let a transit suggestion promise an outcome — the copy
describes the spell, the person decides what rides it.
