# Compass almanac: detailed editorial prototype

September 11–October 10, 2026. Editorial draft for owner review.

## Format

The full edition is a source manuscript, not one oversized social post. Publish a concise month overview and a series of activity carousels drawn from the same edition. Each activity has a reading page, two dates pages with three or four entries each, and a compact reference page. A reader should receive the complete useful answer within the activity carousel. The companion article holds the full month, exact boundaries, and methods.

Five to seven options is an editorial target, not a quota. These provisional selections preserve a distinction between broad astrological themes and narrower openings. Broad periods still require adverse-condition review before publication. Dates and dayparts use fixed CST (UTC−6); Chicago daylight time is one hour later. No quintiles or other minor aspects appear in this revision.

## 1. Move your body

### Reading page

Mars stays in Cancer until the evening of September 27. In an astrological reading, this puts effort in conversation with comfort, familiarity, and the wish to protect what matters. The useful question is what makes a demanding practice feel worth returning to. A home workout, a familiar swimming pool, or training with someone you trust could express that theme. Cancer does not require gentle exercise, and the placement does not determine anyone’s capacity.

When Mars enters Leo, play and expression become more useful themes. Dance, a team sport, a lively class, or practicing a skill in front of others gives effort a visible purpose. Friendly competition can fit, provided the session remains appropriate to the person doing it. These are examples of the symbolism, not separately calculated endorsements of each sport.

## 7. Leave room to rest

### Reading page

Rest has several kinds of evidence in this edition. Pisces, Taurus, and Cancer Moon passages supply broad themes of unstructured time, physical comfort, and familiar surroundings. A waning phase contributes only where the existing rest correspondence uses it. Void-of-course periods are a separate reason to leave some time less scheduled.

For this edition, void of course means the interval after the Moon’s final major aspect to the classical planets before it changes signs. A long void can occupy much of a day or weekend. It does not establish better sleep or medical recovery, and the reading should not make rest conditional on the sky. The practical invitation is to keep fewer outcomes attached to the time, when that is possible.

### Dates pages

- **September 13 afternoon and evening: a long pause in the calendar.** The Moon is void of course from the morning until shortly after midnight. Familiar activities and a plan that can remain open fit the rest reading.
- **September 23, most of the day into the evening: unstructured time.** A long void begins overnight. The Moon enters Pisces later that evening, beginning a separate sign theme for the following days.
- **September 29 evening–September 30 late morning: comfort and a slower start.** Taurus Moon overlaps with a long void and a waning phase. Describe the overlap plainly, without changing the star definition to accommodate it.
- **October 2 morning into early afternoon: a less demanding morning.** The void begins the preceding evening and ends with the Cancer ingress early Friday afternoon.
- **October 3 late morning–October 4 late afternoon: the most prominent rest overlap in this shortlist.** Cancer Moon, the last-quarter phase, and an extended void coincide. A weekend at home with fewer obligations is a concrete expression of those themes. This remains an interpretation, not a guarantee that the weekend will feel restful.
- **October 6 daytime and evening: enjoyment without a deliverable.** The Leo Moon is void after its early-morning Jupiter conjunction. Leisure fits the rest framing without calling the whole day a Jupiter-supported election.
- **October 7 afternoon–October 8, ending early Friday: the longest void in this edition.** Leave space where you can, especially after the specific Mercury contact that begins the void. The earlier creative and study openings cannot be extended through Thursday.
