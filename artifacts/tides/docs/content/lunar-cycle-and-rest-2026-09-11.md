## Method notes

Computed from Compass ephemeris functions and the existing lunar-event scanner. Phase boundaries use exact lunar elongation, not the broad phase-name buckets. No quintiles, planetary hours, crossings, location, or natal factors. No independent ephemeris check has been performed.

The calendar VoC scanner includes Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto, while the ordinary timing VoC predicate uses classical planets. Both methods give the same periods to the minute in this edition, but their definitions should be aligned before treating them as interchangeable elsewhere. The last listed VoC period ends beyond this edition; its actual endpoint is retained for reference.

## Copy direction

Lead with “Sunday afternoon,” “the first weekend of October,” and “Wednesday afternoon through Thursday.” Mark the date span clearly; provide exact clocks only in a caption or expanded reference. Keep sign passages as themes and individual lunar aspects as shorter qualifications. Do not star a whole sign passage based on a single supportive contact.
