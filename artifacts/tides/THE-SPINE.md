# The Spine — what Tides actually teaches

> Draft for the owner to edit. Written as screen-ready copy: each section
> could be an intro slide, a primer page, or the voiceover for one. The goal
> is that a person who reads (or absorbs) this holds the entire app in one
> picture.

---

## 1 · The premise (one slide)

**Time has weather.**

Not every hour is the same kind of hour. You already know this — some
afternoons everything lands, some mornings nothing does. Tides reads the sky
the way a sailor reads the water, and tells you what kind of moment you're
standing in.

It never tells you what will happen. It tells you what the water is doing.
What you sail is up to you.

---

## 2 · The three questions (one slide)

Every reading in the app answers one of three questions:

**What kind of time is it?** — the *character*. Four kinds, set by where the
Moon is: a feeling kind, an acting kind, a building kind, a thinking kind.

**How much water is moving?** — the *charge*. High and rising: lean in. Low
or ebbing: finish things, and rest without guilt.

**Does this suit the moment?** — the *fit*. Everything the app grades — a
task, a habit, a launch window — speaks the same three-step scale:
**a great time · this time will do · against the current.**

That's the whole grammar. Character, charge, fit. Everything else is detail.

---

## 3 · The tide words, explained honestly (primer page)

The app speaks water. Here is the glossary, once and plainly:

- **Rising** — the energy in the day is filling. Starting things goes well.
- **High** — full water. Peak hours; spend them on what matters most.
- **Ebbing** — the water is draining. Nothing is wrong — but momentum now
  favors *finishing* over starting. Edit, close, land, rest.
- **Low** — little in the tank. The honest move is lighter plans. Low tide
  is not failure; it's when the interesting things on the seabed show.
- **Slack water** — the Moon between statements (traditionally "void of
  course"). Beginnings drift. Good for routine, review, and rest.

One rule of thumb carries the whole system: **start on rising water, finish
on ebbing water, rest at low water.**

## 4 · The ladder (the diagram slide — the actual spine)

Time is nested. Each layer turns slower than the one inside it, and the app
is organized top-to-bottom as a depth gauge:

```
  surface   ─ THE HOUR      a planetary voice (~1 hour) — what to do next
            ─ THE DAY       a ruling tone (24h) — what today favors
            ─ THE MOON      the character (~2.5 days) — feel/act/build/think
            ─ THE MONTH     the lunar cycle (~29 days) — seed → peak → release
            ─ THE SEASON    the Sun's sign + the light itself (~3 months)
            ─ YOUR YEAR     one theme per birthday year — where life points now
  seabed    ─ CHAPTERS      the slow planets crossing your chart (years)
```

Read it like water: the surface changes by the hour; the seabed barely moves.
**Small decisions belong near the surface. Big ones belong near the bottom.**
Schedule an email by the hour; time a career move by the chapter.

(Everything in the app lives on exactly one rung: the rail reads top-down,
the Planner works the surface, Aims and the Log work the middle, Star Base
works the seabed.)

---

## 5 · The loop (last slide)

Tides only earns trust one way: **notice, then check.**

Each morning it names the water. Each evening you say how the day actually
felt — one tap. Over weeks, your own log becomes the evidence: which tides
genuinely carry you, which ones you swim against. We call that your fit
with the sky, and nobody can tell it to you — you find it in the Log.

Reflect, don't predict. The sky proposes; your log disposes.

## Appendix A — The character names: alternatives to Deep/Surge/Building/Clear

The current names are metaphor-first, which reads beautifully *after* you
know them and opaquely *before*. Options, with trade-offs:

| Option | Names | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Keep, but always glossed** | Deep · Surge · Building · Clear (+ verb gloss under, every time: *feel · act · build · think*) | Brand voice, already shipped | Two-word learning debt remains |
| **B. Elements as primary** | Water day · Fire day · Earth day · Air day | Zero teaching debt; matches glyph tints; the bilingual layer becomes redundant | Loses the tide voice; "fire day" can read as forecast-of-conflict to newcomers |
| **C. Verbs as names** | a Feeling day · an Acting day · a Building day · a Thinking day | Instantly actionable, no metaphor to decode | Flat; loses both element and water registers |
| **D. Water all the way down** | Deep · Whitewater · Groundswell · Glass | Purest metaphor coherence | Even more opaque than current |

Recommendation: **A or B.** A if the brand voice is the product; B if beta
comprehension is the priority. (C works as the *gloss* inside A — "Deep —
a feeling day" — which is arguably the best of both.)

## Appendix B — Big-project facilitation (the missing persona)

The persona set has PMs (Rachel, Marcus, Deb) but nobody running a
*multi-month program* inside the app. Sketch of persona #33 and what she'd
need — a design direction, not a spec:

**Priyanka, 41 — program manager, runs a 6-month product launch.**
Her question isn't "what should I do this hour" — it's "how do I lay a
six-month arc onto real time, keep twenty workstreams honest, and know
whether we're on pace."

What the ladder already gives her (and no competitor does):
1. **The project is a Guiding Star** anchored to a chapter or her year —
   the seabed rung gives the whole program a season with a real end date.
2. **Milestones = steps timed electionally** — each phase kickoff picked in
   Plan → Begin (the four-step verdict exists for exactly this).
3. **The weekly weave is the sprint planner** — dump the week's tasks Monday,
   let planet-hours place the small stitches and element lanes the deep work.
4. **The Moon is the review cadence** — new moon = planning, full moon =
   review/demo, the built-in biweekly rhythm every agile team reinvents.
5. **The Log is the retro** — readback-by-week with felt ratings is a
   sprint retrospective the team actually fills in.

What's genuinely missing for her (future work, in order):
- **A project view**: one Guiding Star's tasks/steps/sessions on one
  timeline against its chapter (the "burn-down against the season").
- **Dependencies** ("this step can't start before that one") in the weaver.
- **Capacity honesty**: the weaver knows her hours; it should say "this
  month wants 80 hours; the water has 60."
- Multi-person anything (out of scope until "our tides").
