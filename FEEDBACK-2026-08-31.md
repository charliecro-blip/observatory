# Owner pass, 2026-08-31

Captured verbatim-in-substance from a walk through the app. Nothing here is
started unless a commit says so. Ordered by kind, not by the order they arrived.

## The app says something untrue (highest)

1. **Mercury retrograde language when Mercury is not retrograde.** A woven task
   reads "Drafting classically SUITS the retrograde — only the release doesn't."
   Mercury is direct. Also: "any planetary retrograde shouldn't be that huge of
   a hindrance" — the weight is wrong even when the fact is right.
2. **Two back-to-back hours both called the Sun's.** 9:30–10:30 "Sun hour" and
   10:30–11:00 "Sun hour" in the same woven day. One of them is wrong.
3. **A task woven in three times.** "make dr's appt (20 min)" appears three
   times at 3:00 PM under Plan → Already woven in.
4. **Weaving into a day blocked on Google Calendar.** Wednesday is blocked in
   the connected calendar and the weaver scheduled into it anyway.

## Dead ends and flow bugs

5. **"Tap an element to correct it" with nothing to tap.** Both in the Find a
   time sheet and in the woven card for "Prepare Jonathan".
6. **Save time, then asked to schedule again.** Saving a suggested time from the
   Find-a-time sheet still leaves the task prompting to be scheduled in Tasks.

## Language

7. **"the Sun's own hour" / "Mercury's own hour".** Drop "own" everywhere; it
   should read as just the hour.
8. **Log empty state.** "Nothing in the logbook yet. Rate your day or jot a line
   in the journal on Today — it lands here automatically, stamped with that
   day's sky. Completed sessions show up here too." Not good, not helpful.
9. **Shorthand should be decipherable.** "make dr's appt" returned "No clear
   signature from the words alone." The app should read it.

## Visual

10. **Planetary aspect glyphs are too light to be legible** (rail).
11. **The woven-week visualization is bad** — overlapping clipped text in
    "How the week sits".
12. **A small UI oddity on Log** — the "Find and compare days" control.

## Asks (features, not bugs)

13. **Log completed tasks and habits from the Log page.**
14. **Bulk task entry** — dump several tasks at once on the Tasks page.
15. **More than one time option** when scheduling a task, with the astrology
    behind each explained.
16. **Be asked rather than defaulted.** When the words carry no clear signature,
    ask instead of quietly choosing a steady earth block.
17. **Habit emoji.** Is it editable? An emoji picker is wanted.
18. **Habits several times a day** — no such option today.
19. **Select multiple sun times** for a habit.
20. **Optional manual astro mode** — let a user attach an element, sign, or
    planet (or several) to a habit. Must not appear in every case; it is for
    people who speak the language.
