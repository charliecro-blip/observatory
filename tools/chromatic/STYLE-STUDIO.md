# Style studio

Owner preference: visible aspect labels use “conjunct” and “opposing,” rather than “conjunction” and “opposition.” Internal aspect identifiers remain unchanged for compatibility. Apply this wording to future Chromatic copy.

The studio offers Field, Veil, Woven, Glass, Rings, and Weight bar. Pair and solo studies can be built from planet/sign controls; three computed natal fixtures are included. Preferences are separate from symbolic data and can be saved locally. PNG and SVG exports are 1080 × 1350.

Field, Rings, and Weight bar preserve base territory areas. Woven uses quantized thread sampling. Glass pane sizes and Veil optical-density contributions derive from weights before overlap; visible pixel areas are not proportions. Veil is a digital layered-wash study, not a physical pigment simulation or a claim to reproduce an anthroposophical practice.

Open `playground/studio.html` through the existing Vite server, or use the bundled `field-review/style-studio.html` file offline.

## Veil exercises

Curved-edge and straight-edge washes are inspired by Michael Hall's classroom account (https://www.michaelhall.co.uk/blog/?nid=1&pid=3&storyid=248). Rudolf Steiner College Canada describes dilute washes on white paper, with each previous layer dried before the next (https://rscc.ca/veil-painting-with-jef-saunders/). Preserved light is our adaptation of glazing around negative space.

Veil always uses a light paper ground, with dry contours, interleaved passes and restrained multiply compositing. No Gaussian blur or radial glow is used. Layering controls wash count and total optical density; edge definition controls contour irregularity. The weights remain relative pigment budgets, not final area claims. The digital process approximates overlapping transparency, not the spectral behavior of watercolor pigments.
