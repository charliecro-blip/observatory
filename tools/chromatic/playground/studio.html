What stays the same?

The source palette, placement contributions, and aspect stay fixed. Style controls are your preferences. Field, Rings, and Weight bar preserve base areas; Veil, Glass, and Woven mix or overlap colors. Layering applies to Veil, Glass, and Woven; edge definition applies to Veil and Woven. Surface applies to every view.

Veil uses dry-wash studies inspired by Waldorf painting exercises: straight or curved wash edges, repeated translucent layers, and light preserved in the paper. Preserved light is a digital adaptation. It always uses a light paper ground. Natal samples are fixed review fixtures. Your title and saved style stay in this browser.
